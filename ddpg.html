DDPG: how the pieces fit together

DQN picks an action by taking a max over a small menu of actions. A motor torque or a steering angle has no menu, so DDPG splits the job in two: an actor proposes a continuous action, and a critic scores it and tells the actor which way to adjust. Target copies, a replay buffer and exploration noise keep the learning stable.

Exploration noise 𝒩t

OU noiseuncorrelated Gaussian, same σ

OU noise drifts and is pulled back to 0 at rate θ, so it pushes in one direction for several steps. A physical system with inertia actually moves under such a push; white noise mostly cancels itself out.

Soft target tracking

a critic weight θQits target copy θQ′

The trained weight jumps around as each minibatch pulls it a different way. The target copy follows it smoothly, so yi changes slowly. Smaller τ means smoother but slower targets.

Settings

Typical DDPG values are τ = 0.001 to 0.01. The larger default here just makes the lag easy to see.
